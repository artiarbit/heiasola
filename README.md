# HEIA SOLA!

Big buttons that make Spotify play a short clip of a song, for goals at the handball match.

**Open the app:** https://artiarbit.github.io/heiasola/

## For new users
1. Open the link on your phone (Spotify Premium needed).
2. Choose **A – Join Ørjan's app group** (Ørjan adds your Spotify email first) or **B – Set up your own app** (about 5 minutes).
3. Log in. You get the club's buttons and timings automatically.

Tip: in Safari, Share → **Add to Home Screen** makes it open like an app. Buttons you add are saved in that
browser only (Safari and the home-screen icon keep separate copies).

## Before each match
- Open Spotify on the phone that will play, and play any song for a second.
- Open HEIA SOLA! and check the status chip says **Spotify connected**.

## For developers
Plain HTML/CSS/JS modules, no build step. See `CLAUDE.md` for how it's put together, and run
`node tests/smoke.test.mjs` before pushing. Pushing to `main` publishes the site.
