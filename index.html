HEIA SOLA!

HEIA SOLA! controls Spotify on your phone. You need Spotify Premium. Choose how to connect:

A

Join Ørjan's app group

Quickest. Ørjan adds your Spotify account to his app, then you log in. His app has room for 5 people.

  1. Send Ørjan your name and the email of your Spotify account (Spotify → Profile → Account).
  2. When he says you're added, press the button below.
B

Set up your own app

About 5 minutes, no waiting for anyone. You get the same buttons, and you can add up to 5 others to your app yourself.

Show the steps
  1. Go to developer.spotify.com/dashboard and log in with your Spotify Premium account. Accept the terms if asked.
  2. Press Create app. Any name and description work, e.g. "Heia Sola". Tick Web API.
  3. Under Redirect URIs, add exactly this address and press Add:
  4. Press Save. Open the app's Settings, copy the Client ID and paste it here.

To let others use your app: dashboard → your app → User Management → add their name and Spotify email.

Play on

Only devices with Spotify open show up. If yours is missing, open Spotify on it and play any song for a second.

Keep Spotify awake

Phones put Spotify to sleep a few minutes after the music stops, and then it drops off the list. With this on, Spotify plays a silent track on repeat between goals so it stays awake. Each press then loads the song fresh, which is a little slower than a Ready button, but it won't find Spotify asleep.
